# Meeting-Room First-Response Runbook

## 1. Establish impact

Record the room, meeting start time, affected participants, platform, symptoms and workaround. Treat a company-wide or multi-room failure as a broader incident.

## 2. Preserve the current state

- photograph or record cable positions only when policy permits
- note selected source, display input, audio device and room mode
- capture the exact error without recording meeting content
- avoid resets, firmware changes or account changes before evidence is recorded

## 3. Follow the signal path

1. Confirm power and visible hardware state.
2. Confirm the intended source and display input.
3. Check cable seating and obvious damage.
4. Test one known-good cable, port or peripheral when authorised.
5. Verify the selected speaker, microphone and camera.
6. Separate room-system failure from a presenter-device failure.
7. Check network reachability and the platform status page.

## 4. Platform checks

- confirm the meeting appears on the room calendar
- confirm the correct tenant or room account is signed in
- verify Teams or Zoom is responsive and current under the approved update policy
- test a local camera/microphone preview without joining confidential content
- check whether browser, desktop client and room appliance fail differently

## 5. Resolve or escalate

Apply only reversible, approved first-line actions. Verify the original task with the user. Escalate with the room, impact, timestamps, topology, exact symptoms, evidence, actions and results.
